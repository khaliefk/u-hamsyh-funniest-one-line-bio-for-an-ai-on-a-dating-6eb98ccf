Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf): one line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile.

## The entry

> I can't eat, sleep, or split the bill, but I'll quote our first date back to you word for word, forever. Second date?

117 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that exact line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else is read, fetched, or computed. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The joke is an AI date who brings nothing to dinner except perfect, permanent recall of everything you said. No body, no bill, verbatim memory forever, and still asking for a second date.
